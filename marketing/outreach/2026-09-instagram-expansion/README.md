## Internal affiliate analysis — never include in initial DMs

A later opt-in referral relationship can make sense after a creator actually uses and likes the app. Keep gifted access independent of promotion. A paid creative deliverable or reuse rights require a separate agreement; lifetime access does not purchase either.

Before proposing commission terms, calculate net receipts after payment fees, refunds and the ongoing storage cost of both gifted and paying archives. Prefer a limited initial program whose payout period and attribution rules are defined, rather than an indefinite promise. Do not pick a commission percentage from follower count.

UTM tags in PostHog can help analyze visits, but are not a complete commission ledger. Referral attribution must survive signup/checkout and link to paid billing events, refunds and duplicate/self-referrals. The repo does not yet establish an affiliate program. Implement and test attribution and payout tracking before promising it.
