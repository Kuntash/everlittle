## Findings

The Grandma voice-note meme is the clearest discovery leader in this snapshot: 175 viewers and 98.4% of views attributed to non-followers. It reaches more viewers than the similarly aged future-letter Reel (23) or first-night Reel (40). A specific, recognisable family relationship with a playful premise is worth repeating. This single result does not establish humour, duration, or the Reel format as the cause.

The reference carousel has the highest displayed share count (4), but only 18 viewers and four accounts engaged. Owner saves/likes were visibly present; shares may include owner/close-network activity. Four shares is too little to infer a shareable winning format. The carousel recorded zero profile visits, external link taps, comments, and attributed follows.

No inspected post recorded comments or attributed follows. The account now displays six followers, up from four in the prior audit, but these per-post results do not explain the increase. Do not allocate the account-level increase to the Grandma Reel.

The launch image has 66 viewers, more than several Reels. Reels therefore are not uniformly outperforming still content. Public or native interaction totals do not prove signup conversion. Watch time, completion, retention curves, and per-slide drop-off were unavailable; no claims about where viewers stopped are supported.

The prior September 15 audit reported 219 plays / seven viewers for the then-new Grandma meme. The current 253 / 175 observation shows why early reporting and comparable post ages matter. Do not interpret the earlier ratio as evidence of repeated viewing or a viral hit.

## Next test

Keep the three new baby-journal carousels as drafts until explicitly authorized to publish. They share a visual template, five-slide length, and one comment CTA. The prompt topic varies (little words, laughs, bedtime). Evaluate relevant distinct non-owner commenters first; then saves, shares, viewers, and profile actions. Record snapshots at 24h, 72h, and seven days, without deleting earlier observations. Treat a few replies as qualitative learning, not a statistical winner.

In parallel, a future follow-up to the Grandma voice-note premise is justified as a discovery experiment. Use a fresh authentic family situation and compare at the same age. No extra posts are created or scheduled by this report.
