# Everlittle preliminary trademark knockout search

Checked on 2026-08-14. This is an initial founder-level screening, not a legal opinion, a complete
common-law search, or a guarantee that the mark can be registered or used safely.

## Proposed mark and services

- Mark: `EVERLITTLE`
- Proposed domain: `geteverlittle.com`
- Core services: hosted and self-hosted software for private family memories, child timelines,
  photographs, audio, video, letters, invitations, and future-delivery capsules
- Likely important Nice classes for professional review: Class 9 for downloadable software,
  Class 42 for hosted SaaS, and potentially Classes 38, 41, or 45 depending on the final service
  description

## Material commercial uses found

### EverLittle 3D, United States

- Uses `EverLittle 3D` for prenatal 3D keepsakes made from ultrasound scans.
- Its policy identifies the business as `EverLittle 3D` and also refers to it as `EverLittle`.
- It targets expecting and new parents.
- Its product language emphasizes permanent baby keepsakes and personal memories.
- It states that the operator is JDG Manufacturing LLC in California.
- Its privacy policy is dated 2026-04-24.

Risk relevance: high enough to stop automatic clearance. The goods are physical rather than a
family-memory SaaS, but the dominant word, parent audience, baby context, keepsake purpose, and
emotional positioning overlap.

Sources:

- <https://everlittle3d.com/>
- <https://everlittle3d.com/policies/privacy-policy>

### Everlittle, India

- Uses `Everlittle` as a children's clothing storefront and family-facing brand.
- The site says the underlying trade name is `Boss Baby`.
- It targets parents and children in India.

Risk relevance: moderate. Clothing and SaaS are different goods, but the exact name and same
parent-and-child market create confusion and expansion risk, particularly for an India-based
launch.

Sources:

- <https://everlittle.in/pages/about-us>
- <https://everlittle.in/pages/contact>

### EverLittle Co., United States-facing marketplace use

- Uses `EverLittle Co.` for pregnancy announcements and customized baby clothing.
- The marketplace listing says the shop has operated since 2020 and reports substantial sales.

Risk relevance: moderate for common-law and marketplace confusion. The products differ from SaaS,
but the name, pregnancy, baby, and family positioning are adjacent.

Source:

- <https://www.etsy.com/shop/EverLittleCo>

### Everlittle Baby domain

- `everlittlebaby.com` was registered in 2026 and currently appears to be a coming-soon property.
- The registrant identity is private and the future goods or services are unknown.

Risk relevance: uncertain. It reinforces that the `Everlittle` naming space around babies and
families is already active.

## Preliminary assessment

### Result: not cleared for public launch

The search did not expose an obvious exact registered software mark, but it found several active
uses in the same broader parent, baby, family, memory, and keepsake market. `EverLittle 3D` is the
most significant because it is a current US business using the dominant word `EverLittle` and a
closely related keepsake promise.

Adding `get` to the domain does not materially distinguish the underlying product mark. Customers
would still encounter and refer to the service as `Everlittle`.

## Recommendation

- [ ] Do not treat this search as clearance to launch or file `EVERLITTLE`.
- [ ] Ask trademark counsel for a focused knockout opinion covering at least India and the United
      States before investing in the public launch identity.
- [ ] Ask counsel specifically to investigate EverLittle 3D, Everlittle India, and EverLittle Co.
- [ ] Search Classes 9, 42, and any related classes counsel recommends.
- [ ] Decide whether to register `geteverlittle.com` defensively while clearance continues.
- [ ] Do not contact the existing businesses directly before receiving legal advice.
- [ ] Keep alternative product names available in case counsel considers the overlap too risky.

## Practical domain decision

Registering an available domain is not trademark clearance and does not create trademark rights.
If the registration cost is acceptable, purchasing `geteverlittle.com` now can preserve the option,
but development, marketing spend, app-store publication, and a public SaaS launch should wait for a
professional opinion or a naming decision that accepts the identified risk.
