## Spacious v2

The second direction applies the frontend-design skill's refined soft/pastel approach:

- the hero and faithful product preview get substantially more room;
- one continuous “memory path” replaces several competing proof-card grids;
- privacy and family collaboration share one calm sage section;
- pricing is reduced to one focused family plan;
- self-host promotion and navigation are removed from this landing concept;
- three existing Everlittle guides become an editorial journal section:
  **Baby memory journal**, **Letters to your child**, and **Grandparents memory project**.

## Concept

The landing page and authenticated product now share one visual system:

- warm ivory surfaces, pale apricot panels, muted sage accents, and sand borders;
- rounded humanist typography with short, direct copy;
- matte coral primary actions with deep cocoa labels and a solid terracotta lower edge;
- the same sprout brand, camera, microphone, keepsake box, family photography, memory
  cards, navigation language, and archive preview used by the Apricot app;
- clear product-led storytelling without scrapbook textures, editorial serif styling,
  gamification, mascots, fabricated testimonials, or invented product claims.

## Original v1 page story

1. Hero: **“Their childhood is happening. Keep a little of it.”**
2. Proof: photo, voice, and future-letter examples from the same fictional archive.
3. How it works: keep the moment, invite your people, give it back one day.
4. Product proof: a faithful preview of the Apricot Parent experience.
5. Privacy: **“Family memories aren’t content.”**
6. Pricing: the existing $6 monthly / $60 yearly family plan and self-hosted option.
7. Final action: **“Start with the memory you don’t want to lose.”**

The recommended spacious v2 simplifies that sequence to hero, memory path,
privacy/collaboration, one family plan, journal guides, and the final action. It
does not include a self-host section or self-host navigation link.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: `ui-mockup`. Create a polished desktop-and-mobile Everlittle landing-page
concept whose primary references are the approved Apricot Parent screen and Apricot
button-state board. Use the same sprout wordmark, warm ivory canvas, coral raised
actions with deep cocoa labels, rounded humanist typography, pale apricot panels,
sage accents, thin sand borders, natural family photography, and clay
camera/microphone/keepsake-box objects.

Make it a practical product-led SaaS page influenced by Duolingo's clarity and tactile
controls, but with no mascot, streaks, points, XP, childish primary colors, or copied
branding. Show navigation, emotional hero, faithful app preview, three kinds of
memories, three-step explanation, privacy section, existing pricing and self-host
option, and a final CTA. Preserve product accuracy: no fake testimonials, ratings,
user counts, trial claims, AI features, public feeds, comments, likes, search, or
notifications. Avoid dark editorial styling, serif typography, scrapbook collage,
tape, torn paper, glossy gradients, floating cards, and unrelated dashboards.
