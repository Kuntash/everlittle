## Decision rules

Fix checkout or measurement failures before buying more traffic. Stop irrelevant query patterns as they appear. If the INR75 CPC cap gets no useful delivery, report that finding and compare a self-serve Meta creative test or a stronger differentiated landing page before increasing spend. Zero purchases after a handful of clicks is inconclusive; it is not a reason to cut the subscription price or add storage.

Do not publish until live Google tag diagnostics are verified, landing-page claims match the product, and the concrete budget/campaign is approved. A simulated refund remains blocked by Dodo's test wallet balance; production refund processing has not been tested.
