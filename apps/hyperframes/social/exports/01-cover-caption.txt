Three little things worth keeping this week:

1. The word they say in their own way. Write it exactly as they say it.
2. A familiar voice. Ask a grandparent to record a favourite song or family story.
3. An ordinary photo. Add a sentence about what happened just before it.

You don’t need a special occasion to start.

Save this for the next quiet moment.
