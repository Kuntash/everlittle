# Everlittle motion and social design

Canvas: cream #fffbf4. Ink #382d28. Accent terracotta #bf4e35. Sage #e8ebde. Peach #f2dccd. Borders #dfc8b6. Local Nunito Sans variable font matches the current app; bold 800–950 for Reel headlines. Real sprout icon from assets/icon.svg. No invented UI or customer quotes.

Reels: 1080 × 1920, margin 76px, vital content between y170–1595. Headlines 75–130px, body 28–38px. Use high contrast and one clearly named action. Keep the app screenshot at native responsive geometry within its crop. Surface motion uses camera translation/scale and short cursor presses; no generic slide dissolves.

Social images: 1080 × 1350. See social/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md and social/tokens.json. Natural-colour family images are already appropriate and receive no grade.
