# Everlittle content direction

## Objective and evidence

Help parents and relatives preserve everyday family memories, earn qualified Instagram discovery, and turn interest into archive creation. Company voice: warm, specific, useful, no guilt or invented customer testimony. Current evidence is the product and the three launch posts; no customer interviews, keyword-volume research, or performance data have been collected for this plan. Priorities below are hypotheses, not proven audience preferences.

Do not assume the skill's 60/30/10 editorial ratio is an Instagram posting quota. Use it as a future cross-channel starting point once searchable resources are being produced.

## Current comparison and next experiment

- Launch image: product introduction, bio-link action.
- Their Little Voice: 14-second motion demo, family voices hook, bio-link action.
- Grandma stories: 5.04-second UGC-style loop, dense text, save action. The actor is illustrative, not presented as a customer testimonial.

These differ in topic, style, duration and CTA, so do not call the outcome an A/B test. After enough observations, repeat one promising topic with a shorter, more readable overlay while keeping the CTA similar. Record the hypothesis before posting. A five-second loop may generate replays because viewers need more reading time; watch time alone cannot establish usefulness.

## Review process

1. Append metrics at comparable post ages in metrics.csv, with source and evidence. Use Instagram Insights for reach, saves/shares, watch time and follows. Record unavailable values as blanks.
2. At the first weekly review, compare discovery (reach), usefulness (saves/shares per reached account), attention (watch time relative to duration), and intent (profile visits). Report counts with rates and avoid conclusions from tiny denominators.
3. Record Instagram bio sessions and completed signups in channel-metrics.csv from PostHog. Verify the actual signup event definition before counting it. The shared bio link does not support post-level attribution.
4. Write the observation, uncertainty and next single change in learning-log.md. Repeat promising ideas in a different useful format instead of reposting identical assets.
5. Keep the original uploaded file/caption in its published folder. For edits, preserve both versions and note when the live post changed.

## Distribution and attribution

For each owned guide: create the guide first, then one useful carousel and one short Reel or Story from it. Link Stories to the relevant page with a distinct utm_content matching the content ID. Current shared profile link uses utm_source=instagram, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=launch_202609, utm_content=bio. Do not silently change that link per post and assume older-post clicks have become attributable.
