Each version is independently editable under variants/<id>/index.html. The generated source is scripts/build-social.mjs. All use a registered paused GSAP timeline and genuine screenshot assets, not HTML reconstructions of the interface.

Registry discovery: simulated-cursor, zoom-through-transition, parallax-zoom, line-by-line-slide. Adapted motion: cursor press/ripple, focal photo expansion with opposing sibling displacement, camera reframe, waterfall line entrances. Installed source lives in compositions/components. The generic template's tech colours/glitch layers were omitted to match Everlittle's real brand. The waveform is illustrative motion, not a depiction of a played family recording.
