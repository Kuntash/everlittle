## Reusable production recipe

1. Write three hooks around one feature.
2. Use 10–20 seconds of original vertical footage: founder at desk, hands writing a letter, opening a family album, or a commissioned creator. A synthetic character requires a separate video-generation tool; HyperFrames renders edits and typography, not a photorealistic speaking actor by itself.
3. Add concise white captions with a dark outline over safe central space; keep them off eyes and mouths. Use 2–3 short paragraphs instead of the reference's dense wall of text.
4. Cut to genuine Everlittle UI for evidence, then one simple CTA.
5. Render in HyperFrames, then add appropriately licensed music if wanted. Keep voice and music sources documented.
6. Reuse the template and change the hook, one feature shot, and CTA. Test actual signups/activation.
