## 04-note

Image: exports/04-note.png

A letter for your child doesn’t need a big occasion.

Try starting here:
‘You don’t have to do anything extraordinary to be loved.’

Then add one small thing you love about who they are right now. Their curiosity. Their kindness. The way they turn a cardboard box into a whole world.
