# Everlittle landing design direction

Generated design references only. No application code has been changed.

## Design bible

- Concept spine: a living family archive, presented with the care of an heirloom or dossier.
- Palette: editorial cream `#F1F1EC`, deep forest `#17382D`, paper white `#FAFAF6`, and muted oxblood `#8E4F46` used sparingly.
- Type: an expressive Cormorant-like editorial serif paired with a highly readable humanist grotesk.
- Material: matte paper grain, archival rules, restrained shadows, and documentary family photography.
- Shape: restrained 12–16px corner system; no pill-heavy or glass UI.
- Imagery: intimate Tibetan family moments in natural window light, with consistent forest-toned grading.
- Product fidelity: recognizable memories, timeline, voice playback, capsules, family access, readable slugs, and parent-managed child PINs.

## Generation prompt spine

All images were generated as high-fidelity `ui-mockup` references using the built-in image generator. Every prompt required exact readable Everlittle copy, the locked palette and typography above, generous spacing, implementation clarity, and the absence of generic AI treatments such as purple gradients, glassmorphism, floating blobs, dashboard metrics, pill spam, or generic icon-library styling.
